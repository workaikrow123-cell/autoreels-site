AutoReels Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026.

About the application

AutoReels is a personal Telegram bot operated by its owner. It creates short videos and uses YouTube API Services to upload selected videos to the owner's YouTube channel.

Information accessed and used

Google OAuth authorization allows AutoReels to upload videos to the authorized YouTube channel. The application processes selected video files, titles, descriptions, tags and visibility settings. It receives the uploaded video's identifier from YouTube. It does not request or store Google account passwords.

Storage

OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer. Video files and publication metadata are also stored locally. Uploaded video identifiers and links are saved in local JSON files and an Excel publication log.

Sharing

Selected videos and publication metadata are sent to Google and YouTube to perform uploads requested by the owner. The bot sends videos and upload results to the owner's Telegram chat. Google user data is not sold or used for advertising.

Revoking access and deleting local data

The owner can revoke AutoReels' Google account access through Google account permissions . To remove locally stored data, stop the bot and delete its token file, relevant publication JSON files and entries in the Excel log. Local video files can also be deleted by the owner. Deleting local files does not delete videos already uploaded to YouTube; those can be managed in YouTube Studio.
